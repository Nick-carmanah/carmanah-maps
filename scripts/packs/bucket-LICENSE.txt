Carmanah Maps offline basemap files
===================================

These files are offline map data for the Carmanah Maps app. They are cut,
unchanged, from openly licensed sources. If you use them, keep these credits.

basemap/…, packs/…/basemap.pmtiles
  Vector map tiles in the OpenMapTiles schema, from the OpenFreeMap weekly
  planet build named in the file (https://openfreemap.org).
  Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database
  License (ODbL) 1.0: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
  These files are offered under the ODbL as well.
  Tile schema and style © OpenMapTiles (CC BY 4.0): https://www.openmaptiles.org/

terrain/…, packs/…/terrain.pmtiles
  Terrarium elevation tiles from the Terrain Tiles open dataset on AWS
  (https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/), originally by Mapzen.
  Sources and credits: https://github.com/tilezen/joerd/blob/master/docs/attribution.md
  Including: Canada terrain data contains information licensed under the
  Open Government Licence – Canada. SRTM and GMTED2010 data courtesy of the
  U.S. Geological Survey. ETOPO1 data: U.S. National Oceanic and Atmospheric
  Administration. Not for navigation.

imagery/…
  Satellite imagery. Each file's licence and credit is given in catalog.json
  and in the file's own metadata.
